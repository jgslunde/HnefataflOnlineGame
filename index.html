Rules of this game
    AIs

    "NN + MCTS" is trained by reinforcement learning, as AlphaZero was. A neural network (NN) suggests moves and judges positions, and a Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) guided by it looks ahead. More simulations make it stronger and slower. A network plays well only under the rules it was trained with; the line above the board warns when the rules differ.

    "Minimax" (Brandubh only) searches every line a fixed number of moves deep and judges the positions with hand-written rules (heuristics). It plays oddly until it finds a forced win.

    The evaluation is shown from the attackers' side: +1 means the attackers win, −1 the defenders. Move suggestions show each move's share of the search, and its value for the side to move.
